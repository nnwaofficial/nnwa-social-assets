## How to build them in the app (about 15 minutes once, then a minute a day)
1. Check Story archiving is on: Profile > menu > Archiving and downloading > Save story to archive.
2. Save the seven cover images to your phone (from the Library).
3. After the three Start here Stories go out on 1 Nov, open the first one, tap Highlight, tap New, name it "Start here", add frames 2 and 3, then Edit cover and pick cover-start.jpg from your camera roll. Repeat on 1 Nov for "Free tools" with the 09:00 Story.
4. Create the other Highlights the first time a matching Story goes out (Eat well and Courses on 2 and 9 Nov, Protein on 3 Nov, Festivals on 6 Nov, The numbers on 14 Nov), each with its cover.
5. From then on, as each Story above goes live, tap Highlight and add it to its Highlight. Or do it weekly from Archive.
6. Order on the profile follows the most recent addition; to keep Start here first, add to it last on any day you update it.

Facebook Pages have no Highlights, so this is Instagram only.
